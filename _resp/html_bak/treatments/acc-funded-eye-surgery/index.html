Insurance Funded Eye Surgery

Comprehensive Specialist Care to Restore Sight

When the eye is damaged by accident or trauma, vision loss can be complex, often affecting multiple delicate structures simultaneously—the cornea, iris, lens, vitreous, and eyelids. Restoring sight requires more than one kind of specialist; it demands a team approach to total ocular reconstruction.

Scroll Down to Learn More

Multi-Specialist Expertise, The Best Chance for Recovery

We work as a dedicated team of specialists, pooling our expertise in corneal, vitreoretinal, and oculoplastic surgery to provide you with the most comprehensive care possible. Our goal is simple: to give you the best chance of regaining your sight and achieving the fullest possible recovery from trauma.

Why Choose Our Specialists?

Our focus is on precision, safety, and long-term clarity, ensuring you receive the highest standard of care for growths on the surface of your eye.

Unmatched Reconstructive Capability

We have the capacity to replace or repair many parts of the eye that have been damaged, including

The Cornea

Expert treatment, including specialised corneal transplantation (PK, DALK, DSAEK/DMEK) to restore clarity to the front surface.

The Lens

Specialized cataract surgery techniques and custom intraocular lens implants to restore focusing power.

The Iris and Pupil

Advanced surgical reconstruction to repair the iris, restore the pupil's function, and manage light sensitivity.

The Vitreous and Retina

Vitreoretinal microsurgery (vitrectomy) to clear internal debris, repair retinal detachments, and address damage at the back of the eye.

The Eyelids

Oculoplastic surgery to reconstruct the eyelids, tear ducts, and associated structures, restoring function and cosmetic appearance.

Access to Advanced Technology

For trauma and reconstruction cases, standard tools are often insufficient.

Custom Reconstructive Tools

We source customized intraocular lenses, implants, and microsurgical instruments from India, Europe, the UK, and the USA. This ensures we utilise the latest technology for precise reconstruction.

Advanced Diagnostics

We use high-resolution laser scanning and advanced imaging to precisely map the extent of the damage, guiding the reconstructive strategy.

When You Need Us,
We’re Here

We deliver world-class expertise through efficient scheduling, ensuring you
achieve clarity quickly and confidently.

Insurance Affiliated Provider

If your eye injury is the result of an accident and is approved for coverage by Insurance, your assessment, treatment, and surgery are provided through our private practice at absolutely no charge to you.

Expert Insurance Guidance

We manage the Insurance approval process, ensuring rapid treatment without the stress of surcharges or complex claims.

Ready for Specialist Assessment?

Immediate and expert assessment is crucial following eye trauma. Trust our team of specialists to guide your path to recovery.
