Expert Eye Care, Close to Home

Hamilton is the largest centre in the Waikato region and home to our most comprehensive clinic. Located within the Anglesea Precinct in Hamilton Central, our facility brings together experienced specialists, advanced diagnostic equipment and a full range of surgical services under one roof. For patients across Hamilton city and the wider Waikato, this means access to genuine specialist-level eye care without the need to travel to Auckland or Wellington.

Specialist Services Available

Our Hamilton clinic offers the most complete range of specialist eye care services available across our network. Whether you are managing a chronic eye condition, recovering from an injury, or exploring surgical options for better vision, our team provides thorough assessments, clear treatment planning and experienced surgical care.

Serving Hamilton and Surrounding Areas

Our Hamilton clinic supports patients from across the city including Hillcrest, Flagstaff, Rototuna, Chartwell, Claudelands and the surrounding Waikato towns of Te Awamutu, Morrinsville, Huntly and Ngaruawahia. We understand that getting to a specialist appointment takes time and planning, which is why we offer short-notice bookings wherever possible to reduce waiting times for people who need timely care.

Why Patients Choose Eye Surgeons

Our Hamilton patients value the combination of specialist expertise and a calm, unhurried consultation experience. We take time to explain what is happening with your vision, what the options are and what to expect at every stage of treatment. Our team includes surgeons across retina, cataract, glaucoma, corneal and oculoplastic specialties, meaning most conditions can be assessed and managed within the same practice without referral elsewhere.

YAG Laser Treatment

Non-surgical, fast, in-clinic procedures including LPI (to prevent acute glaucoma) and Capsulotomy (to instantly clear vision after previous cataract surgery).
