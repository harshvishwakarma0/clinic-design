Dr Chris Pruet

Specialist in Glaucoma and Cataract Surgery

Dr Chris Pruet is an accomplished ophthalmologist known for his meticulous approach and commitment to patient empowerment. His life’s professional mission is to use the least invasive treatment necessary to protect your vision. This approach, honed through his fellowship at the Mayo Clinic in the USA, ensures maximum efficacy with minimal intervention in both glaucoma and cataract care.

Why choose Dr Pruet?

Discover what sets Dr Pruet apart—trusted expertise, advanced care, and outcomes patients genuinely value.

Specialist Skills & Impact

Dr Pruet provides innovative sight preservation, leveraging experience gained from global leaders like the Mayo Clinic and the UT Health Science Center in the USA. His expertise allows him to prioritise in-office laser treatments and minimally invasive glaucoma surgeries over traditional surgery, leading to a faster recovery and less disruption to your life. As the first surgeon in New Zealand to offer High-Frequency Deep Sclerotomy, you can be assured that you receive care grounded in deep, reliable knowledge aimed at protecting your optic nerve for the future.
